# Exercise 5: CNC Teelight Holder
The fifth exercise was on 3rd June and the subject was about CNC milling.
The task was about creating a candle holder with our own design from a wooden block with three options.
I decided to go with something more unique,so I thought the Yin-Yang symbol might be a good idea.

This is the sketch I did in Inkscape. At first, I tried to come up with two circles merged together, but the edges were not sharp enough, so I tried to use the Inkscape pencil tool and a curve to separate one circle and have two teardrop shapes with two holes for candles.
My whole sketch had four main layers: path 3 as the main circle, path 9 and path 9-5 for candle holes, and path 1 for the curve.
